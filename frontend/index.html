Waste Segregation Guide

Wet Waste

Also known as organic or biodegradable waste.

  • Food wrappers & leftovers
  • Fruit and vegetable peels
  • Meat and bones
  • Tea leaves & coffee grounds
  • Garden waste (leaves, twigs)

Dry Waste

Non-biodegradable waste that can often be recycled.

  • Paper and cardboard
  • Plastic bottles and containers
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Metal cans and foils
  • E-waste and batteries (dispose carefully)
